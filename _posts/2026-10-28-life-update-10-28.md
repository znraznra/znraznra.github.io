As you've noticed, I've been very active for a while around 2wks ago or something due to me trying to make my own Capcut using the very-limited free Claude Sonnet 5.5 with interesting results. I've been very enthusiastic about it but things just kinda fizzled due to me being way too busy with other things. But that doesn't mean that [Kani](github.com/znraznra/kani) is dead. I'm just tying up loose ends.

First off, I've made some progress with helping out a [friend](https://www.youtube.com/@americantheoristbros109) with his passion project (a NIKKE short) by doing its storyboards. I've since managed to finish the first act and I'm not going to lie: it got way too challenging real quick. This also means that I'll need to regroup and think how I'll approach the next 2 acts. I haven't drawn for a long while now and I'm still figuring my way out of it, but I'm enjoying the whole process all in all. I've since bought [Keely Hill](https://keelyhill.com/apps/)'s [Storyboard](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/storyboard-animator/id1326518944) app for iPad, and that's a very useful tool that I've been using to make it. I fully recommend that app for those doing their imageboards/animatics as well.

Next, I've played around with Claude once more and I just gave it a wild idea: write Windows UI using Rust. Now this deserves its own series of posts as it just got way bigger than I anticipated (at least for me). I've built it over COSMIC 'cause I don't think it's wise to reinvent the wheel when there's an open-source base that I can use for the project, and has since been closely testing it for daily use and still finding some issues here and there. I've started this around the time when I took a break from Kani. If you're interested, you can check the [repo](github.com/znraznra/lapin-ux).

Lastly, I've made some progress with my own [channel](youtube.com/@mellyexe) and finished setting up the production workflow. I'm going to be transparent here: this won't be possible without the help of AI tools, and there's no way in hell that I'm going to go full 100% AI to make this. While I believe AI is a really great tool to get things done, I don't think it should act as a substitute for actual human talent. If anything, it should harness it rather than replace it. I've made an automation setup for the hard stuff, used it for resource curation, checking, and translation while I also do my own search and cross-referencing. There are lots of things that AI can get wrong hence why I am also doing my part to ensure that everything is as accurate as possible. I'm also doing the writing, and I made a whole training data of my speaking voice and pattern in Voicebox so that I can automate the narrations and delivery while ensuring that the output sounds exactly like I do. As for video asset curation and creation, there will be zero AI-generated images and videos in there and my own hand-drawn limited-animated narrator stand-in is entirely made by me from poses down to the lipsync and eye behavior. I'm also looking for a way to automate the rig to go along with the narration so that it'll give out a Liv2D-ish feel while not feeling like a Vtuber project.

So that's it from me. I'll be posting again soon.
