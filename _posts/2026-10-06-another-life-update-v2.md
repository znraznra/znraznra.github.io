Right now, my dilemma stands on the following:

- Do I just bite the bullet and move straight to CachyOS?
- Or just install Windows IoT?

Unfortunately, I’ve decided to just go try out Windows IoT instead in hopes of making this laptop faster with a smaller OS footprint as well. I might move the development to my other CachyOS laptop (which is my personal use one) once I refactored up to the natively supported version.

I guess it won’t hurt having a Windows machine to test the desktop client for the RE’d game.
